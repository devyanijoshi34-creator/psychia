Devyani Joshi built PSYCHIA to answer that honestly — without jargon or judgment. Her training is grounded in real classroom experience, not just theory: alongside her BA in Psychology, a completed Diploma in Early Childhood Care & Education, and an ongoing Post Graduate Diploma in Counselling Psychology at Fergusson College, she's spent time actually teaching young children, which taught her that parents don't need to be told what's wrong — they need someone steady beside them while they figure out what's right for their child. How PSYCHIA can help

Two ways to work together, depending on what you need.

1:1 Sessions

Initial session (30–45 min): understanding your child, your family context, and your concerns in depth.

Follow-up sessions (30 min): ongoing guidance and tracking progress over time.

Common areas we work on: sleep & routines, tantrums & emotional regulation, school-readiness, sibling dynamics, screen time, feeding issues, and separation anxiety.

Group Workshops

Small-group sessions for parents (1 hour each), run online via Zoom/Google Meet, or in-person at a rented space or café in Pune.

Current topics:
"Handling Tantrums Without Losing It"
"Building Routines That Actually Stick"
"Getting Your Child School-Ready"
"Positive Discipline 101"
